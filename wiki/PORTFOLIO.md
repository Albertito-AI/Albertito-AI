# Project whitepapers

Twelve public papers prepared for Albert Tan Sin Hon with AI assistance, 2 November 2026. Each distinguishes inspected implementation from ideal future directions and identifies public-interest aims, risks and evidence gates. The private institutional paper remains in its private repository and is not mirrored here.

**Publication verified on 11 November 2026: all twelve listed Wiki papers are live.** Nine enabled but uninitialised Wikis received a minimal Home page; the existing publishers each passed six preservation tests and explicitly reported `PUBLISHED`. CohortFrame, EVT-Albert and Symbolon were already published. The published Whitepaper files were compared byte for byte with the pinned repository sources below. Managed Home sections were also checked, with existing welcome text and unrelated pages preserved.

The earlier `BLOCKED_UNINITIALISED` results and disabled-Wiki observations were historical states and are superseded by this audit. A successful workflow alone is not evidence of publication: the receipts below record the actual publisher outcome. No repository settings, permissions, schedules or publisher code were changed during this completion pass.

Publication currency is separate from research currency. The papers retain their original dates, inspected baselines, rights and limitations; CohortFrame and Symbolon still publish their 2 November design papers. A source match does not establish new research review, independent evaluation or adoption.
