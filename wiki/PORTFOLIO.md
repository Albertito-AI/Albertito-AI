# Project whitepapers

Twelve public papers prepared for Albert Tan Sin Hon with AI assistance, 2 November 2026. Each distinguishes inspected implementation from ideal future directions and identifies public-interest aims, risks and evidence gates. The private institutional paper remains in its private repository and is not mirrored here.

The links below open the versioned papers. Wiki publication is a separate state: see each repository’s `Publish project whitepaper to Wiki` workflow summary and `wiki/README.md`. An uninitialised Wiki needs its first page before the publisher can populate it.
