Collection fit

For advanced postgraduate readers, researchers and specialists in financial law, insurance, secured finance, trusts, Greater China law and institutional information. The book follows premium provenance, policy rights, collateral, company borrowing and trust governance across the Mainland China–Hong Kong interface.

Its doctrinal and comparative method uses constructed transaction files. Separate records of legal positions, value movements and institutional information support a qualified legal opinion. The examples make no empirical claim about prevalence, typicality or motive.

Ready to teach

The separate companion includes eight seminars, 32 practice questions, fictional case activities, lecture slides and an offline study lab. The adoption guide offers a 45-minute introduction, four-week and eight-week pathways, an assessment rubric, an evidence-file assignment, an oral defence and a critical journal club.
