# Library adoption routes

Research checked on 7 November 2026. These are routes for consideration, not confirmations that a library holds, recommends or has adopted the book. No request has been sent through these routes by this campaign.

## A focused adoption approach

Lead with the publisher's free edition, a reliable citation and a concrete teaching or research use. The natural audiences are law librarians supporting financial regulation, insurance, trusts, secured finance, comparative private law and China/Hong Kong studies. Match each suggestion to a collection or course rather than sending the same announcement to every address.

The [library kit](../library-kit.md) provides citation files. The [official companion](https://www.alberttansinhon.com/ibol/) supplies seminars and original teaching activities. Ask an eligible local academic or library member to use a restricted recommendation form. Where a library welcomes external suggestions, provide the DOI, both edition ISBNs, subject fit and open-access link. A free electronic book may need a catalogue or discovery request rather than a purchase request. Record an actual response or catalogue permalink before marking a suggestion accepted.
