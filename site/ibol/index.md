# Insurance-Backed Offshore Liquidity: Open Learning Companion

Albert Tan Sin Hon’s book, published by University of Groningen Press on 6 November 2026, examines insurance, credit and trust governance at the PRC–Hong Kong interface. Its method asks how a fact from one part of a transaction acquires legal significance in another.

[Read the free published book](https://books.ugp.rug.nl/ugp/catalog/book/300) · [Publisher PDF](https://books.ugp.rug.nl/ugp/catalog/download/300/584/1875) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21827/6ac3948159a9e)

The companion contains eight 90-minute seminars, 32 explained practice questions, six fictional evidence cases, a nine-card record-sorting game, a 16-slide lecture and machine-readable citation files. All practice answers are public.
