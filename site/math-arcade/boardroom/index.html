A fresh case

The missing buses

A route schedules 100 buses in each period. In the first, 90 arrive on time and 10 arrive late. In the second, 88 arrive on time, two arrive late and ten are cancelled. A bonus counts punctuality among buses that ran.

Which period earns the higher score? Give the two percentages. Choose a denominator that shows riders what happened, and name the record you would need.

Check your reasoning

The score rises from 90/100 = 90% to 88/90 ≈ 97.8%. From a rider’s perspective, on-time arrivals per scheduled service fall from 90/100 = 90% to 88/100 = 88%. Compare the original published timetable with arrival and cancellation records for the same routes and dates.

Boardroom bonus worksheet

Use the emissions ledger above. Give each player a turn as manager, director and rule designer.

  1. Which proposal earns the manager’s bonus? Calculate each change in own emissions.
  2. Which proposal should a board seeking lower combined emissions approve? Calculate both totals and compare them with the baseline.
  3. Write a new rule specifying whose emissions count, a percentage threshold and the evidence to check. Apply it to both proposals. Aim to reward the upgrade and decline the outsourcing proposal.
