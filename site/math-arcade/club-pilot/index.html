Atlas Open Mathematics

Run a maths circle.

A manager and a board face the same two proposals. Would they choose the same one? Give your group 20 minutes to test the bonus rule and write a better one.

Facilitator's running order

Your 20 minutes

  1. 0–4 min
    Earn the bonusWhich proposal pays the manager?
  2. 4–8 min
    Take the board’s seatCompare the total emissions of each choice.
  3. 8–15 min
    Write a rule in pairsSay what counts and how to check it.
  4. 15–20 min
    Try a fresh caseCan the group apply its reasoning to a bus timetable?

Four-week club pilot

Choose a session format

The pilot is designed for two to four organisers, from 28 November to 26 December 2026, Singapore time. You can use the teaching materials at any time.

Lead your own circle

Open the game on a shared screen or print one handout per pair. The organiser notes include worked answers and a way to assess explanations. Self-run sessions are free.
