Teach with the Maths Arcade

Can a player explain why a digit is forced, a route is shortest or a move survives every reply? Give a pair the same starting position and ask them to defend different choices.

Player sheets

Print one A4 sheet per game. Each pairs an opening with a fresh position and leaves room to record a prediction, a move and its justification. The links below open both printed positions on this GitHub Pages site.
