    Where the moves come from

    These routines interpret descriptions in NewEngen's TikTok trend roundup dated 26 October 2026. The runway routine also draws on NYLON's reporting about Kylie Jenner's walk to JADE's "Angel of My Dreams". This is a dated collection of inspirations, with original robot choreography.

    Sparks' choreography is a robot reading of those written descriptions, so treat it as a homage to each trend. The songs are not included. The beat button plays a drum loop synthesized in your browser.
