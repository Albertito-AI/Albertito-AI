[Read the published book](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24519213) or use [Albert Tan’s teaching page](https://alberttansinhon.com/sport-governance/).

## Practitioner companion

Albert Tan  ·  Revised 25 November 2026

Download an editable file and save a working copy for your organisation. The examples follow Harbour Sport Federation, Foundry Athletic Club, City Women’s League and Borough Sport Trust in England and Wales. Substitute the governing clauses, grant terms, dates, currency and officers that apply to the decision you are making.
