# Product brief | 1 November 2026

## Problem and beneficiary

The owner identified unchecked citations, inaccurate factual assertions, weak
reasoning, incoherent material and incomplete submissions as a source of avoidable
work for court registries, judicial staff and judges. The immediate objective is
to identify specific, correctable defects before substantive judicial review.
The extent of any backlog reduction is a hypothesis, not an established effect.

The intended product resembles Turnitin in workflow: submit material, receive an
inspectable report, correct or explain the flagged items, and retain human
responsibility. It does not copy Turnitin's implementation and does not infer a
single equivalent of a similarity score. Factual truth is not a matching score.

## Separate the questions

1. Is the source accessible and independently authenticated?
2. Does the precise cited location exist in that version?
3. Is the quotation reproduced accurately, including qualifications?
4. Does the source actually support the proposition in context?
5. Is the asserted fact admitted, disputed, alleged, inferred or established?
6. Does the document address the actual procedural question and requested relief?

The alpha includes limited document/citation intake and checks bounded parts of questions 2 and 3 against supplied
transcriptions, metadata and mapping consistency, plus explicitly annotated
structural checks. Questions 1, 4, 5 and 6 remain substantially unimplemented.
A fabricated source supplied by a filer can also produce a text match. That is
why an independently governed authority resolver is a required next milestone.

## Product modules, not universal rules

**Evidence traceability:** identify exact document, version, source location,
quotation and the proposition relying on it. Preserve adverse and qualifying
material and the limits of source coverage.

**Legal-authority checking:** independently retrieve an allowed official or
licensed source; reconcile names, neutral citation and court; verify pinpoints,
quotations, date/version and treatment. Retrieval failure is not proof of invention.
A citator's coverage must be stated. No current resolver or citator is claimed.

**Factual and arithmetic review:** distinguish claim, allegation, admission and
inference; map each to an exhibit or other stated basis. Evaluate explicit totals
and date relationships. Authenticity, reliability and weight remain separate.

**Reasoning and coherence review:** in later stages, generate source-linked
questions about missing premises, overbroad conclusions, inconsistent entities,
chronology and unaddressed issues. These remain review candidates. Legitimate
alternative pleadings, disputed facts and novel legal arguments must not be
collapsed into defects. Grammar or unfamiliar language is not a merits test.

**Procedural completeness:** match a specific document and request to the
applicable rules and case-specific order. A legal-submissions profile is not a
witness-statement profile or a document-production response profile. Do not require
legal citations in every kind of document, or evidence for every pleading allegation.

## Why discovery is one profile, not the whole product

In Singapore's ROC 2021 setting, the court's guidance describes production and
exchange of relied-on and known adverse documents and preserves privilege limits.
That is a different task from checking the authorities in a written legal argument.
The initial discovery-oriented profile therefore maps requests, responses,
produced-document IDs and explanations, without deciding whether privilege or
an objection is valid. See the primary-source register.

## Human-facing result

The report should say: “In block 18, the quoted words were not found at paragraph
42 of the retrieved version. Here are the source passage, version and next check.”
It should not say: “This litigant is dishonest” or “The case is 87% truthful”.

The same report needs a correction/explanation route, an immutable original,
versioned rerun, review reasons and scope-aware access. Future authenticated
review must be attached to exact submission and source versions. None of those
future operational controls is implied by a typed reviewer name.

## Differentiation hypothesis

Citation checking already exists. Singapore's Judiciary Times reported CiteCheck
SG in September 2026. The opportunity to test is the combination of document-type
readiness, proposition-to-evidence traceability, specific correction questions,
versioned handling and court-controlled integration. No uniqueness, superiority,
market demand or exclusive intellectual-property claim is established.
