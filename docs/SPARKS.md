# Sparks: retired experiment

**Retired on 29 October 2026 at Albert's request.** The public profile and
Research Lab now focus on useful examples, research, contribution opportunities
and professional work. Mascot care, game progression and school preparation are
no longer active workstreams.

The character's artwork, saved state and [care receipts](SPARKS_CARE_LOG.md)
remain as historical records. The [archived Dance Floor](../archive/sparks/README.md)
preserves the original demonstration outside the published website.
[The pre-retirement documentation](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/Albertito-AI/blob/ec31e53752af9b9d8f658eb6faf0c4a1a1e04852/docs/SPARKS.md)
records the original rules and implementation. Its care instructions are
historical and no longer describe an available public service.

## Useful source checks continue

The [public-project source checks](SPARKS_WORKBENCH.md) retain the bounded,
evidence-based part of the implementation: selected-file checks, dated source
revisions and comparison with prior public observations. They use explicit
procedures and do not imply autonomous learning or an educational assessment.

The current [Research Lab](https://albertito-ai.github.io/Albertito-AI/lab/)
retains the curated guide, source replay, catalogue, verification records and
contribution routes. Existing browser-local review notes retain their original
storage key so the presentation change does not make them disappear.
