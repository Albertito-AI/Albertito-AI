# Research Lab usability protocol

**Status:** prospective protocol. No external participant session, independent
usability result or educational benefit is asserted by publishing this file.

This protocol asks whether a new visitor can find a relevant source, understand
its stated limits and use the lab's navigation. It is separate from automated
browser checks and from RegNav's existing software and teaching pilots.

## Session

Use the public [Research Lab](https://albertito-ai.github.io/Albertito-AI/lab/)
and record the deployed revision or date shown. A short first round can involve
three willing external visitors with different intended uses, such as a legal
researcher, a developer and an educator. No recruitment or contact is initiated
by this protocol. Agree participation and any recording before a session;
participants may stop at any time.

Ask each visitor to attempt the tasks below without coaching. They may use
their normal device and accessibility settings. Stop a task after five minutes
or when the visitor chooses to stop. Ask what they expected only after recording
what actually happened.

| Task | Observable completion |
| --- | --- |
| Trace a claim through the recorded replay | Visitor identifies the cited passage, compares a changed case and distinguishes a warning from a substantive review. |
| Find one published article and its source | Visitor opens the institutional record and identifies publication status. |
| Find one software example | Visitor reaches its documented demonstration and identifies a stated limitation. |
| Check reuse and citation | Visitor locates the relevant rights statement and a source-specific citation or revision. |
| Find a teaching route | Visitor locates the source-checking materials and recognises their unevaluated status. |
| Use a personal note or export | Visitor creates a non-confidential test note and can locate the resulting download. |
| Find a contribution route | Visitor finds the feedback form without sending it, then locates a bounded project contribution task and its reuse terms. |

For a keyboard or reduced-motion session, also record whether focus is visible,
controls are reachable and motion settings are respected. Do not infer broad
accessibility compliance from one successful session.

## Record actual observations

For each attempt, record the task, device/browser, outcome
(`completed`, `completed_with_help`, `not_completed` or `stopped`),
elapsed time if measured, the point of difficulty and any assistance provided.
Keep a participant's interpretation distinct from the observer's explanation.
Use exact quotations only with permission; a summary does not become a quotation.

Keep raw participant records and consent material outside the public repository.
A public issue should contain only a sanitised, non-identifying account of the
observed difficulty and the steps needed to reproduce it. Do not upload completed
participant forms or screenshots containing personal information.

No observation is recorded until a real session occurs. Blank fields, proposed
sessions, automated checks and the maintainer's own use are not external results.

## Feedback route

Visitors may voluntarily use the
[Lab feedback form](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/Albertito-AI/issues/new?template=lab-feedback.yml).
The visitor reviews the form and chooses whether to submit it on GitHub.
The site does not send feedback automatically.

A useful report states the page, intended task, steps, expected result and
actual result. Include browser or assistive-technology details only where
relevant. Use synthetic examples. Public issues must exclude client documents,
student or participant identifiers, private manuscripts and credentials.

A feedback issue is one person's observation, not evidence of general adoption
or effectiveness. A suggestion is welcome when clearly labelled as untested.

## Review and next action

Group actual obstacles by their cause and impact: a blocked task, a misleading
interpretation or a minor inconvenience. Preserve failures and stopped attempts.
For each proposed fix, identify the evidence, make the smallest useful change
and repeat the affected task with a person who has not been coached through it.

Report sample size and selection, devices, task outcomes, assistance and remaining
uncertainty. Three convenience participants can expose practical obstacles; they
cannot establish population-wide usability or learning benefit. A completed
engineering check remains an engineering result.

No response time, contribution acceptance, payment or research participation
benefit is promised. Follow each linked project's permissions before evaluating
its executable software or contributing material.
